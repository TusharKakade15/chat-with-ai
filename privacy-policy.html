Privacy Policy

PRISM — Multi AI Chat (Browser Extension)

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

1. Zero External Data Collection or Transmission

PRISM does not own, operate, or communicate with any external backend servers, tracking servers, or telemetry endpoints. We do not track browsing habits, clicks, search queries, or usage metrics. We do not collect, monetize, sell, or transfer your personal data to any third parties.

2. Local Browser Storage Only

All discussion threads, custom persona configurations, and roundtable history are stored strictly on your local device via the standard Chrome storage.local API. This data never leaves your computer and can be cleared at any time via the extension interface or by uninstalling the extension.

3. Client-Side DOM Automation (No Credentials Stored)

PRISM operates entirely client-side, interacting with already-open browser tabs that you are signed into (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). PRISM never captures, intercepts, or transmits passwords, authentication tokens, or account credentials.

4. Scoped Permissions & Technical Justification

  • tabs: Used solely to detect, activate, and coordinate ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini tabs during roundtable conversations.
  • activeTab: Used to interact with the target AI tab during prompt delivery.
  • scripting: Used to maintain content script connectivity across browser tab lifecycle events.
  • storage: Used strictly to save local chat history and user-configured personas on your device.
  • sidePanel: Used to display the unified chat workspace within Chrome's side panel interface.
  • Host Permissions: Strictly restricted to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, and gemini.google.com.
